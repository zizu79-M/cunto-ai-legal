Cunto AI

Snap your food. Get the calories instantly.

Cunto AI recognises any dish from a photo, a barcode or a short description, and tracks your calories, protein, carbs and fat against a plan built for you. Available in English, Somali, Swahili, Amharic, French and Arabic.

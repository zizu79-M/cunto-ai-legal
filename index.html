Everything you need, nothing you don't

Built to be opened every day: fast to log, clear to read, and honest about the numbers.

Photo recognition

Point the camera at your plate. The AI names the dish and estimates calories and macros for the portion it sees.

Barcodes and words

Scan a packaged product, or type what you ate in your own language when there is no photo to take.

A plan built for you

Answer seven quick questions and get a daily calorie and macro target that fits your goal and pace.

Progress that motivates

Weight trend, weekly nutrition, a daily streak and badges you unlock along the way.

Six languages

English, Somali, Swahili, Amharic, French and Arabic, with numbers and dates formatted the way you expect.

Private by design

Your data lives on your phone. Create an account only if you want a backup. Delete everything in one tap.

How it works

Three steps, about ten seconds.

Snap

Tap the + button and take a photo of your meal.

Check

See the dish, the calories and the macros. Adjust the servings if needed.

Log

One tap saves it. Your rings update and your streak grows.

Made for everyone

Cunto AI speaks your language and knows your food, from jollof and injera to pizza and sushi.
